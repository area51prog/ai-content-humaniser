# AI vocabulary: flagged words and phrases

No word here is forbidden. Each is a **flag**: if a plainer word carries the same meaning, use the plainer word. Two or three of these in a paragraph is a strong tell. Tier 1 words are the strongest signal.

---

## Tier 1 — measured overrepresentation

Juzek and Ward measured word frequency in ChatGPT output against human-written scientific abstracts and isolated these as the most overrepresented terms. This is the only tier backed by a published frequency study, so treat a hit here as close to conclusive.

`delve` · `delves` · `delved` · `delving` · `showcase` · `showcases` · `showcasing` · `boasts` · `underscore` · `underscores` · `underscoring` · `comprehending` · `intricate` · `intricacies` · `surpasses` · `surpassing` · `garnered` · `emphasizing` · `realm` · `groundbreaking` · `advancements` · `aligns`

The same paper found that readers are now specifically suspicious of "delve," especially in an opening sentence.

## Tier 2 — widely reported single words

Reported across Wikipedia's WikiProject AI Cleanup guidance and multiple editorial style surveys. Frequency evidence is weaker than Tier 1 but the consensus is broad.

**Verbs:** align with · elevate · embark · empower · enhance · excels · foster / fostering · harness · highlight · leverage · navigate · streamline · unlock · utilize

**Adjectives:** comprehensive · crucial · enduring · holistic · key · multifaceted · nuanced · pivotal · profound · remarkable · robust · seamless · transformative · valuable · vibrant · vital

**Nouns (mostly when used abstractly):** ecosystem · interplay · journey · landscape · myriad · plethora · paradigm · synergy · tapestry · testament · trajectory

**Transitions and adverbs:** additionally · arguably · furthermore · moreover · notably · ultimately

## Tier 3 — stock phrases

These are worse than the single words, because they carry no information at all.

- It's important to note that…
- It's worth noting that…
- Based on the information provided…
- In today's fast-paced world / in an increasingly digital world
- Let's dive into…
- Delving into the intricacies of…
- Navigating the complexities of… / navigating the landscape of…
- A testament to…
- Plays a crucial role in…
- Serves as a reminder that…
- At the end of the day…
- The key takeaway here is…
- Here's the kicker / here's the part most people miss
- You're not imagining it
- And honestly? That's rare.
- One thing remains clear…
- As we look to the future…

## Tier 4 — assistant residue

Never belongs in a finished document. Delete on sight.

- Certainly! / Of course! / Absolutely!
- I hope this helps.
- Let me know if you'd like me to expand on any of these.
- Great question!
- As an AI language model…
- As of my last knowledge update / as of my training data
- I don't have access to…

---

## How to use this list

1. Search the draft for Tier 1 words first. Each hit almost always needs a rewrite, not a synonym swap — the word is usually propping up a sentence that isn't saying anything.
2. Tier 2: keep the word only if it is the most accurate one available. "Crucial" is fine when something is genuinely crucial. It is not fine as a default intensifier.
3. Tier 3 and 4: cut, always.
4. Don't replace a flagged word with a fancier synonym. Replace it with a common one, or restructure the sentence so it isn't needed.

**Trap:** swapping every flagged word for a thesaurus alternative produces text that fails the plain-vocabulary check instead. `delve into` → `look at`, not `plumb the depths of`.
